Open in YouTube
  1. Open your playlist on YouTube and copy its link from the address bar (on the YouTube app: Share, then Copy link).
  2. Paste the link in the box below.
  3. A blue box will show your personal Shufflemark link. Bookmark it (press Ctrl+D, or ⌘+D on a Mac; on a phone, use the browser's Share or menu button and choose Add Bookmark).
  4. Whenever you open that bookmark, a random video from your playlist starts playing.
